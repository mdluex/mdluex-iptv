Step 1

Which TV do you have?

Choose your TV and follow the pictures. Most people are done in 5 minutes.

Install on Amazon Fire TV

  1. Get the free “Downloader” app

    On your Fire TV, search for Downloader in the app store and install it.

  2. Allow it to install apps

    Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps → turn on Downloader.

    No “Developer options”? Go to Settings → My Fire TV → About and click your device name 7 times.

  3. Type this address and press Go

    mdluex.github.io/mdluex-iptv/apk

    The newest version downloads by itself.

  4. Choose Install, then Open

    Next time you'll find Mdluex IPTV in “Your Apps & Channels”.

The newest Fire TV devices that run Vega OS can't install apps from outside Amazon's store, so Mdluex IPTV won't run on them.

Install on Android TV / Google TV

  1. Get the free “Downloader” app

    Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Google Play Store on your TV.

  2. Type this address and press Go

    mdluex.github.io/mdluex-iptv/apk

    The newest version downloads by itself.

  3. Allow it, then Install

    When Android asks, allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then choose Install.

  4. Open Mdluex IPTV

    It's in your apps. Prefer a USB stick? Download the Android file below and open it with a file manager.

Install on Samsung

Samsung TVs only accept apps from outside their store when the app is prepared for your TV. The free Apps2Samsung app does that for you.

  1. Turn on Developer Mode

    On the TV open Apps, then press 1 2 3 4 5 on the remote. A “Developer Mode” window opens — switch it On.

  2. Type your phone's or PC's address

    In “Host PC IP” type the IP address of the phone or computer you'll use next (phone: Settings → Wi-Fi → your network). Then turn the TV off and on.

  3. Install with Apps2Samsung

    Get Apps2Samsung (Windows, Mac, Linux, Android or iPhone), find Mdluex IPTV in its list and choose Install. The first time, sign in with a free Samsung account.

    Get Apps2Samsung →

Have a Windows PC? Use the one-click installer instead
  1. Do step 1 and 2 above with your PC's address.
  2. Download the Samsung installer (.zip) below, unzip it and double-click Install-MdluexIPTV.cmd.
  3. Follow the screens — it finds your TV and installs the app. Run it again any time to update.

Install on LG

LG allows apps from outside its store through its Developer Mode app and the free Homebrew Channel. You set this up once.

  1. Turn on Developer Mode

    Create a free account at webostv.developer.lge.com. On the TV install “Developer Mode” from the LG Content Store, sign in and switch Dev Mode Status on.

  2. Install Homebrew Channel

    Follow the Homebrew Channel guide (it uses the free webOS Dev Manager on a computer).

    Homebrew Channel guide →

  3. Add our list and install

    In Homebrew Channel open Settings → Add repository and enter:

    https://mdluex.github.io/mdluex-iptv/lg/apps.json

    Then find Mdluex IPTV in the list and install it.

LG's Developer Mode turns itself off after about 6 weeks: open the Developer Mode app now and then and press Extend.

Step 2

Add your subscription

  1. Open Mdluex IPTV and choose “Add IPTV playlist”.
  2. Type the server address, username and password your IPTV provider gave you.
  3. Save — your channels, movies and series appear, with your subscription's end date at the top.

Easier: scan the QR code shown on the TV with your phone, type the login there and press Send — it appears on the TV by itself, encrypted.

Questions

Need help?

Is it free? Does it include channels?

The app is free. It doesn't include any channels — you need your own IPTV subscription. The Quran and YouTube channels work without one.

A live channel freezes or won't play.

Open Settings → Live TV stream type and switch between MPEG-TS and HLS. One usually works better with your provider.

It says my login is wrong or expired.

Check the server address, username and password with your IPTV provider — only they can fix a subscription problem.

Samsung: Apps2Samsung can't find my TV.

Make sure the TV and your phone or PC are on the same Wi-Fi, and that “Host PC IP” in Developer Mode is that device's current address. Then restart the TV.

How do I update?

Fire TV / Android TV: repeat the Downloader steps. Samsung: Apps2Samsung shows the update. LG: Homebrew Channel shows the update. Your settings are kept.

What's the parental PIN?

It starts as 0000. Change it in Settings → Change PIN.
